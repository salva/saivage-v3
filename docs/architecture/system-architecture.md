# System Architecture

This document is the canonical architecture authority for Saivage v3. Incompatible durable-format cutovers are reset-only; old binaries against current-format state, mixed formats, migrations, compatibility readers, format probes, bridges, and normalization paths are unsupported. Ordinary same-format binary replacement stops the old service and strictly starts the new binary against retained current-format generated state.

## Deployment And Trust Model

Saivage is designed for an externally isolated LXC container. Deployment provides that isolation as the boundary against agent-caused host impact; Saivage neither creates nor verifies it. In-container agents are trusted and may execute shell commands as root.

Internal path, command, and process controls serve operational and API-contract purposes, and runtime/data invariants preserve ordinary correctness and integrity; none contains that trusted root-capable principal. Ordinary workflows may remain constrained despite that capability. External operator authentication and outbound secret non-disclosure remain genuine product boundaries.

## Activation Ownership Architecture

The supervisor owns one `Map<string, CardActivationOwner>` and one guarded synchronous `ownershipTransition`. `CardActivationOwner` is plain activation-local state and the one `CardProcessActor` implementation is the remaining micro-actor, built over a narrow `BaseActor`. `ConversationLLMActor` is a separate direct provider/tool phase owner.

The direct Conversation LLM owner has no `BaseActor`, compiled topology, event queue, `start()`, or actor lifecycle settlement. The transition mutates only in-memory owner phases, parent/child links, currentness, run/token/status, the singular halt identity, exact-run Pause state, and the linked child lease, then validates the graph and emits one coherent invalidation. Analyst intervention admission is a read-only assertion derived from that same Supervisor status, not transition-mutated state. Persistence, process calls, abort delivery, promise delivery, and joins occur outside it and continuations recheck exact identity. Ordinary coordination has no owner repository, factory, registry, second ownership axis, transaction, recovery protocol, or callback bridge. The sole narrow callback exception is the required terminal CardProcess actor-main notification to its exact current/frozen Supervisor owner.

Each configured processor is directly constructed and started ready before its owner is observable. The supervisor constructs one `PlannerChildControlPort` bound to that exact parent activation and passes it through process/node/role composition. Provider code cannot select a parent or read an activation target before delegation. Concrete `CardService` remains the sole root/card reader.

`ConversationLLMActor.toolInvocationContext(outcome)` is the only production constructor of the complete invocation context for autonomous and Analyst calls. `invokeToolForLlm(surface,name,args,context,signal?)` is the singular LLM invocation boundary and has one required-context declaration; no overload, default, fallback, partial context, or role subtype exists. Analyst parses protocol arguments and passes the parsed object directly to that boundary. Lower-level `invokeTool` remains context-optional for direct non-LLM use. Analyst cannot activate children because its surface and dependencies contain no planner port. Valid Analyst and Invocation Service app-log ownership remains; autonomous runtime and Planner dependencies remain free of `appLogs`.

`AnalystSession` is a plain serial phase coordinator. Admission moves one pending restart-confirmation capability into one accepted operation, whose immutable accepted ID identifies ingress and confirmed-restart publication and is distinct from every fresh initial, tool-continuation, and repair invocation source. Its tracker consumer alone installs reusable idle, permanent failed, or disposed settlement and delivers the caller. Reusable non-scheduling outcomes restore confirmation before delivery; successful schedule return alone consumes it. Confirmed restart separates one accepted-ID activation/user batch from schedule entry and fences both after the complete writer boundary and immediately before scheduling. Analyst turns have no cancellation authority or second terminal winner.

The direct Conversation LLM union carries each exact canonical input, provider lease, parked tool context, and concrete child lease in its owning phase. Direct replacement is the sole transition authority. Provider entry creates the invocation lease; canonical writer entry owns publication plus every synchronous observer. Completion then publishes mandatory provider-exchange evidence, synchronously hands the exact terminal input to the enclosing Analyst or Card operation, and settles the direct promise. Analyst outer settlement writes a required terminal-source notice only after an error/model-issue row and evidence. No queued terminal event, lifecycle join, registry, ledger, or mutable latest-input authority exists.

Application teardown directly disposes Analyst and Conversation LLM ownership and joins their owned completion; it does not route through an Analyst-turn cancellation callback, terminal request, transcript notice, or fabricated result. Ordinary tool-result settlement and successful no-continuation `restart_server` result settlement are separate direct effects with the same disposal-after-entry rule: an entered writer appends its one exact caller-supplied result, disposal owns everything after it, the public settlement and Analyst submission reject with the exact disposal reason, internal settlement and joins succeed, and no continuation/provider work is admitted. The `restart_server` case additionally installs no confirmation. Disposal while merely parked revokes later result admission. Entered provider/model-error completion retains `${terminalSource}:error`, mandatory evidence, handoff, required `${terminalSource}:message`, and delivery despite disposal. Only later optional runtime/UI/read-model projection is suppressible. Append, observer, evidence, or handoff failure is preserved unchanged and authorizes no retry, read, fallback, or compensation.

Only positively classified pure pre-effect Analyst preparation rejection is reusable; unclassified, observer, cleanup, and invariant rejection poisons admission, while publication uncertainty reaches fatal delivery before poisoning or cleanup. Disposal and shutdown snapshot exact operation, provider-lifecycle, and child-lease joins, start all joins, and propagate ordinary failure only after all settle. Disposal after ordinary ingress writer entry retains the one activation/workspace/user batch and rejects before reread or provider admission. During confirmed restart, disposal after the two-row publication enters suppresses scheduling after writer return; disposal re-entering from an already-entered schedule preserves the single schedule and scheduled response. These are direct publication/effect fences, not a hidden generic terminal protocol. The supervisor's plain owner, `ownershipTransition`, parent-bound planner port, lease phases, and result winner remain singular. Exact result claim installs direct `continuation_closed` before record publication; an ordinary runtime halt may abandon that near-terminal result and fences every continuation from post-freeze mutation.

Run preparation installs `prepared_root` before recovery/running publication. Owners have only the result/cancel terminal winner; Stop, application close, and terminal actor-main invariant failure converge on one supervisor halt identity. Outcome-unknown publication instead reaches fatal delivery before halt installation. The actor callback verifies exact current authority or frozen-halt membership for ordinary actor-main failure. The ordinary halt freezes the owner graph, closes admission, settles promises, and aborts/disposes owners before joins. Successful quiescence clears process-local authority; interruption/join/termination failure retains the halt and graph. Runtime controls remain delegation-only, and the supervisor remains the sole lifecycle-status and status-derived intervention-admission owner.

The card format is one exact append-only `card.jsonl` stream whose rows are self-contained card-version and terminal tombstone artifacts. Persistence and history share one strict complete durable `CardRecord`; a terminal tombstone is the final row. Operator hierarchy and displayed detail are separate strict generated transport schemas. Dedicated notification, lifecycle, child-link, reorder, and deletion methods construct exact successor rows and append them directly.

Identity derives structural parent/depth but never membership. CardService card-ID inputs fail fast on malformed syntax; well-formed inactive targets retain each operation's explicit not-found result. Exact target reads prove only the reached committed root-to-target path, current artifacts, and parent-child linkage. Subtree reads recursively apply that same strict reached-state proof in committed child order. `getParent()` and `getAncestors()` use exact target scope, while `getDescendantIds()` uses exact subtree scope. Strict `cardRecordSchema` parsing owns root identity/type and unique immediate child shape, while card-ID parsing owns depth and canonical linked traversal owns reached membership. Disposable complete active projections used by `list()` and deletion preflight additionally apply dependency-only `validateParsedCards()` for target existence and dependency cycles. Narrow relationship reads neither open unrelated branches nor validate dependency references merely because a reached card contains them. Analyst project context derives parent IDs only from records returned by its just-completed validated `list()` call; this does not make structural identity an independent membership authority. Operator and tool convenience fields are separately named explicit projections. `CanonicalCardFilesReadModel`, through CardService's canonical projections and descriptor-backed fold, is the sole `.saivage/cards/**` Files path: it returns the selected artifact's serialized virtual document and propagates strict stream-validation failure without generic filesystem fallback. Full-chain recovery remains leaf-to-root with valid partial prefixes, but v2 replay recognizes lifecycle-only stopped transitions.

The supported runtime is Node.js 24 with npm `>=10 <12`, matching `package.json` engines and CI.

## 1. Ownership Model

The runtime is card-centered and alone dispatches work. Startup structurally compiles the complete named-agent catalog and one independent workflow per card type, then binds that immutable artifact once to live dependencies before any actor starts. A card selects only by its immutable type; there is no family or role dispatch and no runtime config lookup or merge.

`compileProjectWorkflows` is the sole deterministic local structural stage. For each card type it compiles declared record metadata, agent record-name globs, `{mode,gate}` requirements, nodes, entries, prompts, and capabilities into explicit drafts; validates reachability, terminal paths, exports, promotion topology, and declaration-only descendant context over each transitive permitted-child closure; then builds and structurally validates one immutable semantic state table. Every requirement must match its node agent's glob for framework-close attribution. Direct `write` is required for both clean gates and `continue + updated`; only `continue + exists` may omit it, and neither `edit` nor `webfetch` substitutes. One startup template compiler exact-selects root-major project/bundled and card/shared agent, process, and direct-fragment artifacts, expands one fragment level under closed host policies, freezes agent tokens, eagerly renders process text, resolves ordered tool references through the provider-owned global/card catalog, and expands each selected route/profile into immutable `orderedModelIds` using the strict array-form equivalence and direct-failover order. It performs no provider credential, MCP, process, network, or generated-state work. Process prompt IDs remain on node and transition contracts, while the workflow's `processPrompts` map solely owns selected process-prompt bodies. `init`, `reset`, ordinary start, and `start --create-runtime` all receive that singular result before generated-state publication or deletion. `bindRuntimeWorkflows` is the startup-only second stage: it discovers the selected Analyst plus distinct agents from direct node states and installs one `agentBindings` map containing each structural contract, selected tool set, retained complete-surface capability request, and explicit-ID Registry candidate chain. Bindings sit beside and never rebuild the singular state tables. Server composition passes only the bound artifact to runtime actors; real invocation binds scope closures without source-config selection, and reconfigure's candidate compile is validation-only and is never installed.

Node execution reads each process-prompt body by ID directly from the exact compiled workflow's `processPrompts` map, with no runtime registry or global re-lookup.

All node-to-node and terminal edges share one acceptance owner. `AgentNodeExecution` prepares every required closed/open projection before mutation, terminal routes claim the existing winner before close, and updated streams close once in requirement order. The returned immutable close projections are the only source for accepted URLs, transition context, and terminal exports. `CardProcessActor` retains the latest accepted result per node only in activation memory and applies `current` or graph-reachable `latest_node` promotion at the terminal route. A primitive-classified `PublicationOutcomeUnknownError` from a close reaches fatal delivery before result acceptance, Supervisor halt, lifecycle publication, or parent settlement. Ordinary pre-commit close errors remain ordinary node failures.

One plain `CardActivationOwner` holds each mapped activation in exactly `prepared_root | child_admission | active | settling`. The sole `CardProcessActor` directly extends the narrow `BaseActor` and owns configured process position, activation tracking, exhaustive guarded activation settlement, LLM ownership, node transitions, and local join. BaseActor's generic one-shot terminal hook rejects current/future lifecycle waits with the exact latched error and forbids pump restart. `AgentNodeExecution` owns one node's compiled named-agent surface, prompt, correction, record, notification, and cleanup behavior. The supervisor's one map, terminal winner, and nullable halt record are the sole publication, cancellation, currentness, and freeze authority. Durable running without an exact owner fails fast.

Supervisor pre-admission and project-root preparation are the only root activation boundary. Startup uses required concrete `actorStore`; there is no second root reader. Root ownership is installed before recovery or running publication. Child activation exists only through the exact parent-bound port and invocation lease. Every processor is started ready before owner visibility and activated only after successful running publication.

Cancellation claims the exact active suffix synchronously, preserves already-done descendants, aborts only under cancellation authority, publishes deepest-first, and joins one cached owner settlement. Natural release removes the exact owner/relationship from the one map before caller delivery. This is process-lifecycle authority, not a persistence lock, queue, transaction, generation, or currentness mechanism.

Card status behavior is intentionally operation-named rather than one terminal taxonomy. The cards-owned `card-status.ts` implementation exports exactly four small predicates/effects, and `status-api.ts` is their singular cross-package entrypoint: `canCreateChildInStatus` admits backlog, changed, running, blocked, and stopped; `canCancelCardStatus` admits every status except done and cancelled; `acceptsCardNotifications` admits backlog, changed, running, blocked, and stopped; and `analystRecordEditEffect` preserves backlog/running/stopped, reopens blocked/done/failed, and rejects changed/cancelled. Caller scope, root, runtime, type, dependency, draft, and publication gates remain at their owning surfaces. Planner creation requires every dependency to be an existing immediate sibling under its current parent; Analyst creation delegates an existing-active-card dependency rule to CardService and additionally rejects a running parent. Dependency completion is activation admission, not creation admission.

The Analyst's first-class `reopen_card` is a narrow audited application mutation, not a generic transition API. It admits only intervention-ready blocked/done/failed targets and delegates the target and eligible resting ancestors to existing `propagateChange`; it adds no planner permission or operator card action.

Status-to-process admission has one nullable mapper: backlog maps to BACKLOG, changed to CHANGED, blocked to BLOCKED, stopped to STOPPED, and running/done/failed/cancelled to null. It grants no transition authority. The supervisor invokes it only after exact owner absence and one admission read; rejection precedes owner/processor construction. Stopped uses dedicated `activateStopped()` publication.

Each card type publishes one immutable state table as its only compiled topology. Every transition carries one target state ID, reentry, and a semantic variant containing no duplicate destination locator; node or terminal identity comes only from looking up that target state. Configured-outcome transitions own edge prompts and terminal promotion/exports. `CardProcessActor` executes that table one node at a time and retains accepted result-by-node memory only for the activation. The narrow `BaseActor` reads the same table through only transition, parked, and terminal mechanics; there is no projected actor definition or retained node/edge index. Same-node reentry clears the settled task before entering the new node. CardProcess joins every frozen lane and chooses failure in fixed order: pre-join cleanup, LLM actor order, tracker, then lifecycle. App-log publication failure sends no event and makes Supervisor start the singular runtime halt; actor-main failure reaches the same halt through the narrow exact-owner callback while remaining a distinct failure policy.

Every accepted edge uses one record-acceptance algorithm before transition. Node entry applies `clean` or `continue` only to exact requirements and captures numeric head baselines after preparation. Acceptance checks non-empty `exists` or post-baseline `updated`, then closes required open records in declaration order followed by other activation-written open records in sorted name order, recording only close-return URLs. Direct tools and framework close are distinct capabilities under the same compiled name-glob authority. Graceful non-accepted settlement discards activation-written drafts before generic invocation-surface cleanup; correction loops retain them. Once finalization begins, ordinary failure stops at the committed prefix without compensation or retry; primitive-classified uncertainty permits no later record or cleanup effect. A hard crash loses the activation-local set: exact requirement entry may later handle a requirement draft, but a free draft is only directly readable/reusable by a later same-name authorized activation or removed by whole-state reset. No discovery, catalog, adoption, or selective repair exists.

Other status decisions remain exact and local: running-chain membership is `status === 'running'`; dependency completion is `status === 'done'`; descendant completion is done-or-cancelled; activation settlement is done/failed/blocked with a distinct cancellation branch; deletion and Analyst reorder reject running; and projected start admits only backlog/changed/stopped. `CardService.editCard()` singularly owns Planner metadata-delta pruning and coupled publication: no effective delta returns unchanged; a real backlog/changed/stopped delta appends only metadata; and a real blocked/failed delta invokes public `setStatus('changed')` for its nested fresh admission/publication before appending metadata from the returned card. Running/done/cancelled reject. Planner reorder directly targets its current parent and may therefore reorder a running parent; Analyst reorder is intervention-ready, globally addressed, and rejects a running parent or any running member of a requested child subtree. Both return `changed:0` without card/status publication, propagation, or notification for identity order; the Analyst invocation still receives its ordinary successful audit settlement. A real Planner reorder has no propagation, notification, or control-action audit, while a real Analyst reorder performs changed propagation after the same parent-only canonical reorder.

Default allocation is a configured workflow/API boundary for trusted agents. Analyst has global create/reorder/reopen/cancel/delete maintenance plus generic record mutation but no child activation or Planner metadata edit; Planner has immediate-child create/edit/cancel/activate and current-parent reorder but no delete/reopen; Reviewer and Executor have no default structure tools. Their child ceilings permit creation only for Analyst and Planner and grant neither dispatch nor reopening. Runtime dispatch still exists only through the active exact parent-bound port and invocation lease.

Cancellation preserves the same caller separation. Planner delegates an immediate child owner-first without reading the target or subtree. Analyst rejects root and preflights the whole requested subtree, denying the entire request when any member is done or cancelled. Supervisor alone claims active suffixes and publishes deepest-first while preserving noncancellable stored descendants. Analyst deletion instead preflights running members and survivor dependencies and publishes permanent tombstones; it is not cancellation.

Generic record mutation likewise has conjunctive admission rather than tool-name authority: the current URL must resolve to an active linked card, card-scoped agents may target only their own card, the acting agent needs a matching `record_writes` glob and every operation-required tool, and Analyst additionally needs a supported `analystRecordEditEffect` plus no open workflow draft. Content checks and card-agent-draft versus Analyst open/edit/close effects remain operation-owned.

## 2. Card Identity And Discovery

Operator Card reads remain direct exact-path projections owned by `CardsReadModelService`, split into hierarchy, displayed detail, compiled descriptors/current record artifacts, card/record history, selected versions, and diffs. Hierarchy proof folds each reached card stream once, omits tombstoned links, and never follows grandchildren. For each reached card, the read model projects required `permitted_child_types` directly from its startup-compiled workflow into the hierarchy summary and fails if that workflow is absent. Metadata history lists rows from the same complete fold. Explicit history selects the exact requested row. No cache, repository, generic Files bridge, or alternate validator exists.

The browser mirrors those four authorities. Parent-keyed slices carry explicit undiscovered/loading/error/loaded-nonempty/confirmed-leaf view state. The Cards tree combines the hierarchy summary's compiled `permitted_child_types` with that local state: only a configured-nonempty, non-confirmed-leaf row has disclosure. This rules out impossible discovery without claiming descendant knowledge or introducing a separate config or card-type client authority. Deep reveal advances one accepted ancestor slice at a time. Selected detail owns no descriptors. Descriptor and `(cardId,name)` content owners are abortable and selection-scoped; reconnect reloads descriptors for the new server epoch and refreshes only mounted accepted mutable resources. Generic Files is separate and remains available for explicit file browsing.

Card identity is `project` or one-to-twelve lowercase alphabetic hierarchical segments selected from a deterministic parent-local spreadsheet sequence. One schema-owned maximum governs identity parsing, parsed-state validation, mutation, canonical Files path parsing, and browser route reconstruction. Every create starts at `a`; exact candidate namespace creation claims the segment, and only candidate `mkdir` `EEXIST` advances to the next segment. Collision handling never lists siblings or inspects the candidate. Parent, depth, namespace, exact card/record streams, and named-agent conversation paths are forward-derived. `CardRecord` has no `position`. Backend `listChildren`, logical-path, reorder behavior, and web nested/direct-child projections follow each reached parent's committed cumulative `children` array order; active projections omit retained tombstoned links without changing that order.

One non-root attempt exact-reads and validates its linked parent, then CardService computes resulting depth. Over-limit depth is rejected immediately so the explicit safeguard wins over ordinary parent admission and request-type/dependency errors. In-limit creation next applies ordinary parent workflow/lifecycle admission, resolves the selected child workflow, and requires an empty child `permittedChildTypes` set exactly at depth twelve. That complete initial sequence precedes dependency processing, fresh-parent reads, namespace claim, publication, linking, and freshness effects. It then validates existing dependencies and exclusively claims `.saivage/cards/project/children/<s1>/.../children/<sn>/` for the first candidate whose direct `mkdir` succeeds. `publishInitialChildCard()` constructs the complete card and brief from exact child input; `publishInitialProjectCard()` separately constructs the complete root from only title and brief. Dependency state is immutable after version 1. Existing-card-only dependency creation is cycle-free by construction, so mutation-era service/index cycle preflight does not exist; `validator.ts` alone retains whole-loaded-graph dependency-cycle rejection. The later parent append alone grants linked membership. A failed claimed namespace remains consumed and ignored.

Successful-path visibility begins only after the confirmed parent-link append. Readable initial child streams before that link are not membership. Publication uncertainty authorizes no read, retry, reconciliation, or artifact inspection. Card-only readers derive the bounded linked chain and fold each reached exact card stream. Complete initial publication separately proves the bootstrap record stream. A terminal tombstone terminates traversal; exact-known conversation access remains available. Unlinked namespaces are ignored orphans.

## 3. Direct File Persistence

Saivage-owned persistence is direct stateless synchronous file I/O by the domain or actor owner:

- `card-files.ts` owns deterministic child-namespace claiming, nonempty first publication of `card.jsonl`, one-envelope row appends, the strict complete fold behind exact current/history/version/diff selection, cumulative child links, and terminal tombstone rows. Physical stream snapshots stay internal to strict reading and never surface as Files metadata.
- `authored-record-files.ts` owns one exact `record-<stem>.jsonl` stream per record. Card creation publishes the bootstrap record's closed first row from `runtime:bootstrap`; optional records have no stream until their first mutation, and a missing optional record stream classifies empty. Every operation directly reads and validates the exact stream call-locally and appends exactly one row; there is no carried head token, no empty canonical publication, no dynamic initializer, and no historical normalization. An undeclared valid name uses deterministic generic metadata, and first publication's exact-target existence check is the sole claim. There is no parent creation, ensure/EEXIST branch, retry, cleanup, or inspection. No proof survives as later write authority, and no registry, slot enum, sibling scan, or brief-specific branch exists.
- `canonical-conversation-artifacts.ts` owns the strict version-index, segment-genesis, segment-envelope schemas and their integrity commitments. `conversation-file.ts` owns direct canonical publication, strict reads, genesis-derived validation seeds, compaction publication, and startup-only truncation of an unterminated suffix after full retained-prefix validation for each deterministic named-agent session. It never shortens an index or searches semantic checkpoints. Ordinary v1 and compacted genesis make every current segment independently valid; compaction commits N+1 by immutable-file creation followed by index replacement. The canonical conversation validator owns semantic grouping.
- `contracts/app-log.ts` owns the strict three-lane `{type,data}` union for event, control-action, and provider-exchange rows. `persistence/app-log.ts` owns the sole direct synchronous preparation/validation/serialization/append boundary and strict reads, using the current project root directly. Its one private call-local semantic validator enforces global logical-ID uniqueness across all lanes for complete reads and prospective existing-plus-candidate admission. Domain producers supply preparation closures and invoke their narrow direct post-commit effects only after successful publication.
- `provider-exchange-log.ts` is the singular latest-provider-exchange selection owner. Each invocation strictly acquires the complete provider-exchange lane once and reduces it once by exact session ID, greatest timestamp, then greatest attempt index, retaining the earlier physical row on an exact tie. Singular exact lookup delegates to that same map implementation; no second filter/sort comparator exists.
- `ResolvedConfigAuthority` owns selected YAML document reads, the sole card-type source-resolution boundary, internal effective validation, and config mutation orchestration. Its source schema accepts an optional complete `card_types` map; omission resolves to the bundled `classic` template's `card_types` data, and the deleted `card_type_set` key fails strict validation as an unknown key with no fallback or merge. The authority resolves the source once into the required complete effective `card_types` map before parsing and compiling. Its source schema accepts the same nested string-array `models.equivalents` representation consumed by effective configuration, with no legacy preprocessing. The browser-safe declarative schemas layer separately owns the strict outbound effective-config schema/type, which contains the resolved complete map and no source-only form. Contracts and backend projection implementation both depend on that schemas package root; schemas and contracts never depend on backend config or redaction implementation. `config-file.ts` owns canonical atomic replacement.
- `auth-profile-file.ts` and `project-identity.ts` own their single canonical files.
- `runtime/lock.ts` is the exceptional process-exclusion boundary only.

One strict `cardRecordSchema` defines every current `CardRecord`, including card-version, embedded history-snapshot, and tombstone-final fields. The card-stream row schema is the sole structural parser for each raw row at read and write boundaries; `validateCardStream()` consumes only those typed parsed rows and performs identity, history, transition, immutable-field, child-link/reorder, ordering, and tombstone validation without another Zod parse.

The current singular operation grammar has no common patch writer, caller-selected context, optional terminal companion, generic dependency mutation, or broad transition API. `commitActivationOutcome()` is the sole running-to-done/failed/blocked publisher and derives lifecycle, both required status companions, and notification clearing from the non-cancelled outcome and one settlement timestamp. Generic status owns only backlog/blocked/changed-to-running, blocked/done/failed-to-changed, and the six admitted cancellation sources; recovery and stopped activation own their disjoint pairs. Every actual delta has deterministic order and recomputed summary. The eight discriminated history families carry fixed row-local provenance. Replay scans no child stream and proves only row-local facts: notably child-link is runtime-owned, while reorder replay proves the private complete same-membership full-order primitive, not the public operation's historical active-set admission.

The bottom-layer card schema keeps its notification-format invariant locally and imports no card behavior: complete done, failed, and cancelled records require empty `pending_notifications`, while backlog, changed, running, blocked, and stopped may retain them. This exact schema refinement intentionally duplicates the higher-layer notification predicate rather than creating a `schemas -> cards` dependency.

Planner child admission exact-reads the linked target and each dependency in declared order. It never invokes list/tree/index APIs and retains no authorization state. Linked-tree operations start at the exact root and recursively follow committed `children`; their disposable projection validates active dependencies and hierarchy and is discarded with the call. Multi-root deletion uses that one fresh projection to form a subtree union, reject permissions and surviving dependents, and topologically order dependent-before-dependency plus child-before-parent tombstones. This graph is call-local write admission, not persistence coordination.

The complete, duplicate-free cumulative parent `children` snapshot is the sole linked-membership and semantic-order authority, including retained tombstone links. A real active reorder uses one fresh operation-local parent/active-child projection, requires a duplicate-free permutation of the active IDs, and constructs one next parent snapshot with requested active IDs first and all retained non-active links afterward in stable prior order. It appends one parent version and never writes child streams. An active-order no-op has no append or effect, even when retained links are interspersed in the old complete order. Parent-local stream validation admits only a changed, same-length, same-membership permutation declared as a children-only reorder, with no other local field change; identity order, membership change, and piggyback mutation fail. Validation compares consecutive parent snapshots only and carries or derives no active/tombstone classification in history. Thus both the old and new complete parent rows are valid committed prefixes without reorder coordination.

Planner activation validates schema and immediate-child identity, reserves the exact invocation lease, and delegates through the bound parent port. The supervisor validates parent and lease before I/O; owner absence alone authorizes one admission/dependency/status read. It constructs and starts a ready processor, silently installs owner/relationship/lease, performs one running publication, then transfers currentness and activates the processor. Exact same-lease joins perform no I/O.

Dependency and non-activatable-status rejection precede owner and processor construction. They cause no running publication, relationship/currentness change, or execution work and add no scheduler, traversal, propagation, or cancellation behavior.

Every replaceable publication—configuration, project identity, and the conversation index—calls `replaceFile`. Card and record streams never replace: each mutation appends one strict envelope to the exact stream. Crash-left temporary files remain ignored forever.

All file, directory, append, and lifecycle-lock creation uses ordinary Node defaults filtered only by the process umask. No mode argument, chmod/fchmod, permission probe, or repair exists.

Growing canonical JSONL files use one result-bearing `appendEnvelope()` API: `appended` means write, fsync, and close all succeeded, while `missing` means only that the initial exact-path open returned `ENOENT` before a descriptor existed. Every caller handles both results explicitly. First publication separately calls `lstat` on the exact target exactly once: any successful result is ignored and means already published, only `ENOENT` permits one unchanged replacement attempt, and every other error propagates. It never opens or classifies an existing target, so files, directories, symlinks, FIFOs, sockets, and devices take the same present branch. App log alone may use append `missing` for one first-publication attempt; card, record, and nonempty conversation owners fail fast because their previously read append target must still exist.

Append acquisition uses `O_WRONLY | O_APPEND | O_NOFOLLOW | O_NONBLOCK` and descriptor metadata, never a path probe. The descriptor must be a regular file before any write. Every successful open has exactly one close attempt: an earlier stat/validation/write/zero-progress/fsync error takes precedence over close error, while a lone close error propagates, and close is never retried. After validating and serializing its incoming row, the app-log owner strictly reads the exact existing stream without mutation; incomplete or complete malformed data fails. The app-log's singular semantic validator then checks the complete existing rows followed by the parsed candidate for global logical-ID uniqueness before append acquisition. Duplicate rejection writes no candidate and emits no post-commit effect. The unchanged direct append primitive never reads or truncates existing bytes, and append-open `missing` remains the sole selector for one first-publication attempt.

The singular generic growing-file whole-file reader opens the exact path with `O_RDONLY | O_NOFOLLOW | O_NONBLOCK`, requires a regular descriptor, reads all bytes, and strictly decodes and parses every envelope. Empty, incomplete, invalid UTF-8, malformed-envelope, and invalid-row content fails without mutation. App-log initialization, explicit reads, and append admission all use this reader; initialization alone treats exact `ENOENT` as absent, and a present empty stream fails unchanged. Agent summary reads do not use transcript readers: they obtain `started_at` directly from canonical conversation index metadata.

The inferred strict `LoggedEvent` union and runtime event schema agree on exactly `runtime_diagnostic`, `runtime_actionable_error`, and `mcp_tool_invocation`. The actionable-error envelope is strict. ## 6. Notifications And Terminal Arbitration

There is no websocket resource, timer, watcher, polling, or process cache. Dashboard consumes only the store and renders quiet zero, honest unavailable, or a nonzero safe latest banner. Its link selects `/agents/<session>?entry=<marker>`; Agents validates the marker query, fetches only the exact session, and highlights/scrolls only that exact row or reports it missing. Rendered marker text is synthetic and contains no provider response.

Named incompatible durable-format changes require stopping each affected service, running the matching current built reset, and then starting that binary. Directory-owned parent-local spreadsheet claims, the exact two-kind card stream, card records without `position`, complete parent-owned `children` membership/order, immutable card type across every card version, the ordered summary-group payload, minimal strict `applied_policy`, and marker-first/no-preamble Analyst source contract are such cutovers. Successful candidate `mkdir` consumes its segment even if later work fails, and only the parent's committed cumulative `children` snapshot grants and orders membership. Generated streams containing old `card-child-reservation` rows fail strict current-format reads. Release format knowledge determines that a cutover applies; startup never samples, probes, classifies, or normalizes retained state to infer compatibility or exempt an affected deployment. Every later card version and tombstone retains lifelong type, so role/session projection cannot change; changed-type streams fail strict validation. Old generated rows, changed-type card histories, and pre-marker or mixed Analyst histories fail strict reads, and there is no migration, marker backfill, compatibility classifier/renderer, selective repair, or mixed-version operation. Ordinary same-format binary replacement instead stops the old service and strictly starts the new binary against retained current-format generated state, without reset. `resetOwnedGeneratedRoots()` in `src/persistence/layout.ts` is the singular reset ownership and initial-publication-presence contract: `.saivage/cards`, `.saivage/agents`, `.saivage/logs`, and `.saivage/work`. Initial publication requires all four exact roots absent; retained partial state names its first blocker and requires stop, current-built reset, then retry. These generated roots are removed as whole trees without descendant inspection. Durable configuration and operator inputs under `.saivage`, including identity, credentials, prompt overrides, skills, and instructions, remain outside that ownership boundary, as do source and canonical documents.

Content-policy retry/refusal rows, typed refusal BLOCKED results, and the required error-exchange terminal-output field extend that reset-only inventory. Generated-state test and E2E fixtures are reset to the singular current schemas; no omitted-field fixture, compatibility transport, legacy blocked/tool shape, migration, or normalization path remains.

The `.saivage/locks` namespace is the exceptional lifecycle-exclusion boundary, not reset-owned generated persistence. Reset acquires the exact canonical `runtime.lock`, removes the four complete roots, publishes the new `project` root card while still holding the lock, and then uses exact-owner release. A pre-existing exact lock blocks deletion. Lock siblings and every other outside-boundary path are neither discovered nor cleaned. Mixed-version operation and rollback against current-format state are unsupported.

Saivage file persistence does not guarantee no data loss. Interrupted external effects may repeat; notification context may duplicate; concurrent auth refresh may overwrite another change; incomplete card namespaces and publication temps may remain forever. Exact canonical corruption fails clearly for operator repair or reset rather than automatic recovery.

## Universal publication fatal delivery

`PublicationOutcomeUnknownError` is the dependency-neutral signal from direct file
owners, and `ApplicationFatalPort` is injected from application composition. The port
uses synchronous fd-2 output followed unconditionally by `process.exit(1)`. Catch
guards rethrow the same instance until the earliest concrete owner can deliver it.
`BaseActor` stays persistence-neutral through a no-op fatal-task hook; its sole
production subclass, `CardProcessActor`, supplies fatal delivery. `ConversationLLMActor`
is a direct phase owner, while `AnalystSession` and `SupervisorRuntimeApi` remain plain
coordinators. `AnalystWsHandler` owns ordinary WebSocket turn and queue rejection
boundaries; publication uncertainty exits from the consuming fatal boundary before it
can send a response.

Replacement uses exclusive same-directory temporary publication and becomes uncertain
at rename. The startup conversation-tail owner becomes uncertain at `ftruncate`.
Lifecycle-lock first creation syncs its parent directory and treats the created empty
namespace as known. JSONL append uses `missing` only for exact append-open `ENOENT`;
process output requires its prepublished exact file and leaves every open error direct.
All canonical write loops permit only proven-zero-transfer first-write `EINTR` and
suffix-only completion after short writes.

Process logs use no `WriteStream`, pipe, retained descriptor, or writer lifecycle.
Each data callback performs one synchronous exact-file append. Presentation settlement
joins managed-group absence with both captured readable drains, and all three
ProcessRunner termination surfaces join only report `stopped` identities before
returning. Reader-local projections remain disposable; recovery writes remain
append-only and do not create indexes, body rewrites, or recovery coordination.
