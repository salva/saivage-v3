Look for specific recurring patterns leaving the same acceptance gap: repeated retry/repair without substantive delivery, growing prerequisites or disconnected patches and passing checks without required integration, cycles sustaining unnecessary machinery, or unresolved feedback to a terminal owner unable to act. Local progress is positive evidence, not automatic exemption from this assessment. An ordinary review mentioning a terminal card is insufficient.

Missing/partial evidence does not prove absent progress or commits. Record revisions and repeated citations alone prove neither convergence nor futility; neither do elapsed time, execution ordinals, volume, difficulty or a red test. Healthy slow work, research narrowing uncertainty, truthful red cycles and deep review remain legitimate; establish the concrete recurring gap and a useful next action for the responsible owner before advising, otherwise take no action.

Normal advice:
When such a newly actionable pattern is evidenced, use ordinary admission to send one concise `queue_notification` with `urgency: "normal"` to the nearest responsible nonterminal card reporting `planning_target: true`. Identify the card, observed window, relevant progress, recurring pattern, unchanged acceptance gap and any terminal implementation owner. Ask its Planner to reassess approach/decomposition, reconsider the need for machinery and simpler alternatives where the evidence warrants it, or coordinate correction with the actual owner. Do not patrol complexity, demand another review, notify the terminal card, broadcast, prescribe automatic reopening or assert strategy failure as fact.

Respect reasoned Planner disagreement, correct advice when later evidence changes it, and repeat no equivalent notice without new value. Lack of observed handling is not new value. Normal advice does not interrupt descendants and may wait for an existing recipient opportunity. Non-progress alone warrants neither urgent escalation nor a duplicate normal-and-urgent pair.
