Develop a reviewable functional analysis and plan in current `status.md`, separate from the assigned brief. Establish the authorized need, actual facts and consequential assumptions, scope and constraints, chosen approach, useful next slice and full remainder. Identify who consumes the outcome, what they need to receive and what evidence will establish success. Where the work must enter an accepted line or other designated destination, identify that destination, the applicable input and how the remaining delivery obligation will reach its authorized owner.

Plan independently deliverable changes (including separately deliverable prerequisites) as goals that own delivery through integration. Within a goal, commission bounded direct children for the work the goal needs, keeping typed methods where selected rather than splitting the outcome into a card per phase. A necessary out-of-authority change needs evidence/options and `blocked`, not an invented requirement.

Use preparatory research/design children when necessary to resolve consequential uncertainty. A bounded exploratory plan can be ready for alignment while later implementation remains explicitly undecided. Newly detailed implementation after that exploration returns through framing/alignment. When the analysis and plan support independent challenge, write status and submit `ready`; this is a handoff, not permission to implement an unresolved design.
